### Downloadable Forms

[Authority form](https://www.medibank.com.au/content/dam/retail/brochures-forms/Authority_Form.pdf)

*   Authorise someone to act on your behalf when dealing with Medibank.

[Accident claim form](https://www.medibank.com.au/content/dam/medibank/docs/forms/Accident-Form.pdf "Medibank Accident Claim Form")

*   Make a claim under Accidental Injury Benefit (also known as Accident Override), or if you have had an accident within two months of taking out or upgrading your hospital cover.

[Australian Government Rebate (AGR) application form](https://www.medibank.com.au/content/dam/retail/marketing-assets/form/medibank-australian-government-rebate-form.pdf)

*   Apply for the Australian Government Rebate on private health insurance as a premium reduction.

[Claim form](https://www.medibank.com.au/content/dam/retail/brochures-forms/mpl593-medical-extras-claim-form.pdf)

*   Make an extras, medical, hospital or bonus claim to post to Medibank, or submit in-person at a Medibank store.

[Direct debit form](https://www.medibank.com.au/content/dam/retail/brochures-forms/Direct_Debit_Form_Bank_Account.pdf)

*   Set up direct debit from your bank account or credit card to pay your premiums.

[Lifetime Health Cover (LHC) exemption form](https://www.medibank.com.au/content/dam/retail/brochures-forms/LHC_Exemption_Form.pdf)

*   Apply for exemption from the Lifetime Health Cover loading.

[Health support benefits approval form](https://www.medibank.com.au/content/dam/retail/brochures-forms/health-support-benefits-approval-form.pdf)

*   Apply for health support benefits for certain services recommended by a health practitioner intended to manage an existing health condition.

[Pre-existing condition form](https://www.medibank.com.au/content/dam/retail/brochures-forms/PEC_Medical_Certificate.pdf)

*   Help us to assess if the condition you are going to hospital for is a ‘pre-existing condition’.

[Statutory declaration form](https://www.medibank.com.au/content/dam/retail/brochures-forms/statdec.pdf)

*   Complete a statutory declaration statement.

[Suspension of membership request form](https://www.medibank.com.au/content/dam/retail/brochures-forms/suspension-form.pdf)

*   Going overseas or have your circumstances changed? Download this form to apply to place your membership on hold.

[Travel and Accommodation Claim Form](https://www.medibank.com.au/content/dam/retail/marketing-assets/form/medibank-travel-accommodation-claim-form.pdf)

*   Manual claim for to be used to access Travel and Accommodation benefits on eligible hospital product.
