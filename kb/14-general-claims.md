## How to make a claim online

#### 1 Step one

Register or[login to My Medibank](https://members.medibank.com.au/)on our website, or by using the[My Medibank App](https://www.medibank.com.au/health-insurance/using-your-cover/mobile-apps/).

#### 2 Step two

Select 'Make a claim' and follow the simple instructions to submit your claim.

#### 3 Step three

Medibank will then pay any applicable benefits directly into your bank account or send a cheque in the mail.

[](https://itunes.apple.com/au/app/medibank-mobile/id465576269?mt=8 "Download My Medibank for Apple")[](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=au.com.medibank.phs&hl=en "Download My Medibank for Android")

### Want a little more help with your claim?

Our claims guides and information page is here to walk you through claiming for the most common services, like ambulance, health appliances and subscriptions, with simple step-by-step instructions and clear information.

[Visit claims guides and information](https://www.medibank.com.au/help/claims/)

## What claims can't be submitted online?

Depending on your cover, the following claims cannot be submitted online. However, these claims can be submitted offline using one of the methods listed below:

*   Claims involving compensation or damages  
*   Individual claims associated to more than one individual
*   Miscellaneous services

## How to submit an offline claim:

**Send your claim along with the claim form to:**  
 Medibank Private Limited  
 GPO BOX 2984  
 MELBOURNE VIC 3001

**For extras claims:**provide Medibank with your completed claim form and the invoices and/or receipts.

**For residential medical claims:**lodge the bill with Medicare as a paid, or unpaid claim using the[Medicare two-way claim form](https://www.humanservices.gov.au/individuals/forms/ms001 "Medicare two-way claim form").

Alternatively, you can make a[claim with Medicare](https://www.humanservices.gov.au/individuals/forms/ms014 "Medicare claim form"), then with us, by providing Medibank with the Medicare Statement of Benefits.

**For Overseas Workers or Overseas Visitors Health Cover medical claims:**for medical services that can't be claimed online, mail your receipts directly to Medibank with a completed claim form.

**For Overseas Student Health Cover claims:**please see the[OSHC claims page](https://www.medibankoshc.com.au/How-to-make-an-online-claim/ "OSHC claims page").

## [](https://www.medibank.com.au/health-insurance/using-your-cover/claim/)Frequently asked questions

[Why do I have waiting periods when I’ve just upgraded my cover?](https://www.medibank.com.au/health-insurance/using-your-cover/claim/#accordion1faqaccordionsfaq_517c)

After upgrading your cover, waiting periods will only apply for any new services, or for increased benefits or annual limits. If you need to claim during the waiting period, you will receive the same benefits as your previous level of cover, provided you have served all waiting periods on the previous level of cover. Log into the [My Medibank app](https://www.medibank.com.au/health-insurance/members/mobile-apps/) and head to the Out of pocket estimator section to estimate future claim expenses.

[I’ve given Claim Consent to someone else on my policy, why can’t they see my claims online?](https://www.medibank.com.au/health-insurance/using-your-cover/claim/#accordion2faqaccordionsfaq_517c)

Unfortunately, viewing claim information after giving Claim Consent is not available online through My Medibank. To request personal information regarding a claim please [contact us](https://www.medibank.com.au/contact-us/).

[Why can’t I set my partners bank account for claims?](https://www.medibank.com.au/health-insurance/using-your-cover/claim/#accordion3faqaccordionsfaq_517c)

We take the privacy of our members seriously, and it’s for that reason members are unable to edit financial information for partners, spouses and any dependants over the age of 16.Don’t forget, other members on your policy who are over 16 can create their own My Medibank account and update claim payment details on the go both online and through the app (remember the email address should be different to others on the policy). Get started [here](https://members.medibank.com.au/#register-tab).

[Why can I not see my partners extras limits?](https://www.medibank.com.au/health-insurance/using-your-cover/claim/#accordion4faqaccordionsfaq_517c)

We take the privacy of our members seriously, and it’s for that reason we only show your limits and those of dependants under the age of 16. Don’t forget, other members on your policy who are over 16 can create their own My Medibank account and view their extras limits on the go both online and through the app (remember the email address should be different to others on the policy). Get started at [here](https://members.medibank.com.au/#register-tab).

[I need to make a claim, why can’t I send through all claims online or email them to you?](https://www.medibank.com.au/health-insurance/using-your-cover/claim/#accordion5faqaccordionsfaq_517c)

Some services require extra paperwork, and it is for that reason not all claims can processed electronically. See further up this page for a [list of services that are claimable online](https://www.medibank.com.au/health-insurance/using-your-cover/claim/#claimable-online) and a procedure for other services that are required to be claimed with supplementary paperwork.

[I have an Extras Cover that includes a Bonus, why can’t I see my limits when I log into My Medibank?](https://www.medibank.com.au/health-insurance/using-your-cover/claim/#accordion6faqaccordionsfaq_517c)

Currently “Product bonus’” are not visible in My Medibank. Please [contact us](https://www.medibank.com.au/contact-us/) for assistance regarding limits.
