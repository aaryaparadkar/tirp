## We offer two overseas health products for students

**I am looking for**

#### Hospital

Pays benefits towards accommodation and medical costs for included medical procedures in hospital. This includes charges for treatment you receive from doctors, specialists, surgeons and anaesthetists.

*   Comprehensive
*   No excess
*   Accident and emergency department facility fees+

#### Medical

Pays benefits towards medical services in and out of hospital.

*   Inpatient medical services
*   General Practitioner (GP) consultations
*   Other out-of-hospital medical services (blood tests & x-rays)
*   Allied Health services billed with an MBS item number^

* * *

#### Pharmacy

Pays benefits towards the cost of eligible prescription medicines. You will be required to pay a contribution of $30 towards the cost of each eligible prescription medicine before we'll pay any benefits.

*   Annual limit of $500 per member (up to $1,000 per membership)#
*   Benefit amount of up to $70 per item

* * *

+A ‘facility fee’ is an amount that may be charged by private hospitals for attendance in their accident and emergency department. ~If anyone on your membership is admitted to hospital for a service Included under your cover and a partner, immediate family member, carer, or next of kin (referred to as a ‘boarder’) needs to stay in hospital with them, we’ll pay towards the cost of accommodation and meals raised by the hospital for that ‘boarder’.
