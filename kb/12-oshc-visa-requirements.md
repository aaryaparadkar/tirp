### Student Visas

#### What visa do I need to study in Australia?

There is now only the Student visa - [subclass 500](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500) - no matter if you’re coming to study at university, TAFE, or high school. If you hold a closed student visa - subclasses 570-576 - the following information is for you too.

If you are planning to study in Australia for less than three months, you may be eligible for a visitor visa.

#### What kind of health insurance do I need for my student visa?

To be granted a Student visa for Australia you must purchase [Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC)](https://www.privatehealth.gov.au/health_insurance/overseas/overseas_student_health_cover.htm).

There are some countries with special agreements with Australia, and people from those countries may be exempt from needing OSHC. [Find out if you are from one of those countries here.](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/individuals/services/medicare/reciprocal-health-care-agreements/when-you-visit-australia)

#### What does OSHC cover me for?

At the least, an OSHC plan must provide adequate medical and hospital cover, plus emergency ambulance transport and limited pharmaceutical cover. This will help with the costs for some doctors and hospital visits, should you need them. [Here’s a detailed list of what to look for in an OSHC plan](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/temporary-graduate-485).

#### What doesn’t OSHC cover me for?

[Here’s a detailed list of treatments not covered in a basic OSHC plan](https://www.health.gov.au/resources/collections/overseas-student-health-cover-oshc-resources?utm_source=health.gov.au&utm_medium=callout-auto-custom&utm_campaign=digital_transformation).

OSHC generally doesn’t cover you for services such as dental, optical or physiotherapy. But if you’d like to be covered for these services, you can take out ancillary or extras cover. You can [talk to Medibank](https://www.medibank.com.au/overseas-health-insurance/contact-us/)'s overseas health cover team about the best extras cover for you.

### Working Visas

#### What visa do I need to work in Australia?

If you’re coming to Australia to work temporarily you will most likely need to apply for a working-type visa, the most common types are the [Skills in Demand visa 482](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/skills-in-demand-visa-subclass-482), [Temporary Graduate visa 485](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/temporary-graduate-485), or [Working Holiday visa 417.](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/work-holiday-417)

#### What kind of health insurance do I need for my work visa?

To be granted a 482 visa or 485 visa for Australia you must purchase a suitable level of health cover. Medibank offers [Working Visa Health Cover](https://www.medibank.com.au/overseas-health-insurance/working-visas/), which is 100 per cent compliant for these visas.

Purchasing health insurance isn’t mandatory for 417 visas, but it is recommended you get cover anyway.

There are some countries that have special agreements with Australia, and people from those countries may be eligible to receive subsidised healthcare once they arrive. [Find out if you are from one of those countries here.](https://www.humanservices.gov.au/customer/enablers/health-care-visitors-australia)

#### What does working visa health cover provide?

At the least, a working visa health cover plan must provide coverage for in-patient medical services and hospital cover, plus ambulance cover. This will help with the costs of some doctors and hospital visits, should you need them.

#### What doesn’t working visa health cover provide?

Most working visa health covers don’t cover you for services such as dental, optical or physiotherapy. If you want any of these services covered, you can purchase a higher level plan, or take ancillary or extras cover. You can [talk to Medibank](https://www.medibank.com.au/overseas-health-insurance/contact-us/)'s overseas health cover team about the best extras cover for you.

[Medibank offers four different plans](https://www.medibank.com.au/overseas-health-insurance/working-visas/) for people working temporarily in Australia. Each one offers unlimited emergency ambulance cover+, and all meet the minimum requirements for your visa. Choose from basic cover with the bare necessities, up to comprehensive cover with included extras. Plus, if you buy online you’ll get your visa health insurance certificate immediately.

### Visitor Visas

#### What visa do I need to visit Australia for a holiday or business?

If you’re coming to Australia for a visit or for business, you will most likely need to apply for a [Visitors visa 600](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/Visa-subsite/Pages/visit/600-visitor-landing.aspx), [an eVisitor visa 651](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/evisitor-651)or an [Electronic Travel Authority 601](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/electronic-travel-authority-601). [Here’s a full list of visitor and business visitor visas](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/visitor-600).

#### What kind of health insurance do I need for my visitor visa?

Some visitor visas may require you to hold adequate health insurance while you’re in Australia. This is known as visa condition 8501.

For example, the Sponsored Parent visa 870 and 590 Student Guardian visa are subject to visa condition 8501, meaning visa holders must maintain adequate health insurance for their stay in Australia. A 600 Visitor visa can also be subject to visa condition 8501 in certain streams or circumstances.

For other visitor visas, you generally don’t need to hold health insurance to be granted the visa. However, the Department of Home Affairs strongly recommends visitors arrange private health insurance, as most visitors to Australia don’t have access to Medicare and may need to pay the full cost of hospital, medical or other health care treatment themselves.
