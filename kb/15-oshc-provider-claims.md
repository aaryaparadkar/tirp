# How to make a non-resident claim

3.   Non-resident claims

## [Non-resident medical claims](https://www.medibank.com.au/providers/claims/nonresident-claim/#accordion_par_1510207729_1)

**For visitor/working visa members:**

You can claim non-resident GP appointments through HICAPs.

For all other outpatient services, simply fill out and mail the [Medibank claim form](https://www.medibank.com.au/content/dam/medibank/pds/MPL5930719-Claim-Form.pdf) as per the instructions on the form.

**For overseas students:**

Outpatient medical claims for Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) go through the OSHC direct billing portal. The OSHC direct billing portal allows you to submit claims directly, verify memberships, and receive your remittances.

To register for the OSHC direct billing portal, contact us on [1300 130 460](tel:1300130460) or web messaging to express your interest.

Note that when approved, you will be registered with both Medibank and ahm.

## [Non-resident hospital claims](https://www.medibank.com.au/providers/claims/nonresident-claim/#accordion_par_1510207729_2)

**Resident and Overseas Worker and Visitor policies**

Please send completed[HC21 form](https://www.privatehealthcareaustralia.org.au/resources/provider-resources/national-private-patient-hospital-claim-form/) and invoices to:

Medibank Hospital Claims

GPO Box 9999

Melbourne VIC 3001

Please allow 21 days for payment. To check the status of a claim please contact the Provider Advocacy Team on [1300 130 460](tel:1300130460) or use our web messaging service.

**OSHC policies**

Please send completed[HC21 form](https://www.privatehealthcareaustralia.org.au/resources/provider-resources/national-private-patient-hospital-claim-form/) and invoices to:

OSHC

Locked Bag 1006

Matraville NSW 2036

Please allow 21 days for payment. To check the status of a claim please contact the Provider Advocacy Team on [1300 130 460](tel:1300130460) or use our web messaging service.

## [Non-resident outpatient and emergency department claims](https://www.medibank.com.au/providers/claims/nonresident-claim/#accordion_par_1510207729_3)

Please send[Medibank claim form](https://www.medibank.com.au/content/dam/medibank/pds/MPL5930719-Claim-Form.pdf)and invoices to:

Medibank

GPO Box 2984

Melbourne VIC 3001

Please allow 21 days for payment. To check the status of a claim please contact the Provider Advocacy Team on[1300 130 460](tel:1300130460)or use our web messaging service.
